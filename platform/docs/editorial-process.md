# Biweekly editorial process: programmes and news

Every 14 days (and sooner for a known deadline), by an INOK editor, with an authorised administrator publishing. Monitoring stays off; this is a manual process.

1. **Review official sources.** MITRA, TEKUN, SME Corp, SME Bank, AIM, KUSKOP and the Ministry of Finance, plus recognised press for discovery only.
2. **Find changes.** New schemes, changed amounts or criteria, opened or closed rounds, new announcements.
3. **Verify against official evidence.** A press report is a lead, not proof. If the agency page cannot be read, say so in the entry and keep the status "not confirmed".
4. **Check the details that mislead most:** repayable financing versus grant; allocation or budget line versus an open call; dates and the date of the source.
5. **Enter it in the review queue.** Programmes through the source and proposal workflow (`gov_submit_candidate`); news as drafts with publisher, source link and dates. Original summaries only.
6. **A reviewer approves** in Admin, with a written reason. Nothing is published automatically; approval marks a programme reviewed, so only approve what step 3 supports.
7. **Record** the sources, retrieval date and decision (the admin screens write the audit trail).
8. **Archive** closed programmes and outdated news instead of deleting them.

Switches: Publishing may stay on for reviewed content; Monitoring and Notifications stay off until their operational checks pass and the administrator approves.
