# Legal applicability matrix (2026-11-16) — for management and counsel

**This is not legal advice and does not say INOK is compliant.** "Verified" means the primary text was read in this session; "Not verified" means only secondary summaries or an official index page were seen, and counsel must check the source. "Required" is law; "Recommended" is good practice. Sources read: Act A1727 (Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024, pdp.gov.my), Personal Data Protection Commissioner's Circulars 1/2025 (appointment of DPO) and 2/2025 (data breach notification), P.U.(A) 449 (Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transaction) Regulations 2024, Gazette 24 Dec 2024), the Commissioner's pages on the 13 data-user classes and the data-protection principles.
