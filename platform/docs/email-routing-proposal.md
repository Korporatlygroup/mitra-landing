## 2. Exact changes Email Routing makes
Cloudflare adds them when you press "Onboard domain"; the dashboard shows the exact values first, and I will copy them into the change record before you approve. Per Cloudflare's documentation the onboarding adds, on the **root** `inok.my`:
1. **MX records** pointing to Cloudflare's mail servers (`route1`, `route2`, `route3` under `mx.cloudflare.net`), with priorities generated by Cloudflare;
2. **one TXT record for SPF** authorising Cloudflare Email Routing;
3. **one TXT record for DKIM** for routed mail.

Nothing is changed or deleted on existing records. Propagation takes 5 to 15 minutes. Sending through Resend from `mail.inok.my` is unaffected because it uses its own subdomain records.

**Optional, separate step (not part of routing):** change `_dmarc.inok.my` to `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@inok.my; fo=1` once `dmarc@` exists.

## 4. Steps after approval (about 20 minutes)
1. Snapshot the zone: Cloudflare > `inok.my` > DNS > Records > Export (a copy of today's records for rollback).
2. Cloudflare > Compute > Email Service > Email Routing > Onboard domain `inok.my` (shows the records first) > Done.
3. Destination addresses: add each recipient above; each recipient verifies through the emailed link.
4. Routing rules: one rule per address (Action: Send to an email).
5. Test each address from a different mailbox; confirm arrival and the sender display; check spam folders.
6. Optionally update `_dmarc.inok.my` as in section 2.
7. Record the result and fill the legal-page placeholders with the final addresses (the documents stay drafts).

## 5. Rollback
Email Routing settings > disable (or delete the three MX and two TXT records by hand using the exported copy). Existing records are untouched, so rollback returns to today's state. Messages already forwarded stay delivered.

## 6. Limits to know
Forward-only: staff cannot reply as `support@inok.my` from the forwarding itself. Replying from that address needs either a Gmail "send as" through Resend's SMTP (this requires the root domain `inok.my` to be verified in Resend as a second domain; the Free plan allows 3 domains) or a later move to a Google Workspace alias domain. Cloudflare does not charge for Email Routing.
